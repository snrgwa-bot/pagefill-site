Support

We read every message and usually reply within two working days.

Email us at essentialdevteam@gmail.com

The fastest way to report a problem is from inside the app: open Settings, tap Send Feedback and choose Problem. You can include your app and iOS version and a diagnostic log. Nothing is sent until you tap Send.

Common questions

How do I restore my Pro subscription?

Open the Pro screen in Pagefill and tap Restore. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to your device's Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions, choose Pagefill and tap Cancel Subscription. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I fill in a form?

Open the document and choose Fill. Tap a field to type and tap a box to tick it. Then use Save or Share at the top.

How do I sign a document?

Open the document, choose Sign and draw your signature or pick a saved one. Then tap the page where it should go. You can drag, resize and rotate it afterwards.

How do I delete a saved signature?

Open the Signatures tab and tap the red delete button on the signature, or touch and hold it and choose Delete Signature.

Is redacted text really gone?

Yes. Applying a redaction rebuilds the marked pages, so the hidden words no longer exist in the saved PDF. Always check the result before you share it.

Where are my exported files?

After a tool finishes you can Share, Save to Photos or Show in Files. Exports are also kept in the Pagefill folder in the Files app.

Request a feature

Have an idea? Tap Send Feedback in Settings and choose Idea, or email essentialdevteam@gmail.com. Many of Pagefill's tools started as user requests.
