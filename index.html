Questions.

Is anything uploaded?

No. Pagefill opens, edits and saves documents on your iPhone or iPad. There is no Pagefill server, no account and no analytics. Text recognition, the AI summary and the redaction finder all run on the device.

Does it work offline?

Yes, every tool. Fill, sign and convert on a plane or anywhere without signal.

Where is my signature kept?

On your device, in the Signatures tab. Delete any saved signature there whenever you like.

Is a signature from Pagefill legally valid?

Pagefill places an image of your signature on the document. Whether that counts depends on the document and where you live, so check with the person or office asking for it.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later.

How do I get help?

In the app, open Settings and tap Send Feedback. Or write to essentialdevteam@gmail.com. More answers are on the support page.
