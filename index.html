Features

Everything a PDF needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Open a file, pick a mode, get it done. Pagefill keeps the tools you use most one tap away.

Fill and sign without printing anything.

Tap a field and type. Tick a box. Draw your signature once and place it exactly where your finger lands.

  • Smooth, natural ink that looks like your real signature
  • Move, resize and rotate anything you place
  • Check and cross marks, and “Sign here” stamps

Redaction that actually removes the text.

A black box on top is not enough. When you apply a redaction, Pagefill removes the hidden words from the file itself, so nobody can copy them back out.

  • Select text, draw a box or use the censor marker
  • Find phone numbers, ID numbers and faces automatically
  • Option to keep the rest of the page searchable

Mark it up like paper.

Pens, highlighters and notes for quick reviews, plus pro shapes when you need to explain something clearly.

  • Highlight, underline and strike through real text
  • Arrows, clouds, callouts and dimension lines
  • Colour notes that sit in the margin, not over your text

FAQ

Good questions.

Does Pagefill work offline?

Yes. Every tool runs on your iPhone or iPad, so you can fill, sign and convert documents on a plane or anywhere without signal.

Is my signature stored anywhere?

Your saved signatures stay on your device. You can delete any of them from the Signatures tab at any time.

Does redaction really remove the text?

Yes. When you apply a redaction, the marked pages are rebuilt so the hidden words no longer exist in the file. They can't be copied, searched or recovered from the saved PDF.

Which devices are supported?

Pagefill runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later.
