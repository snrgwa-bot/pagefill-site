Privacy Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

Pagefill does not collect your personal data. Your documents, signatures and settings stay on your device.

This policy explains how Pagefill (“the app”), made by Essential Dev Team (“we”, “us”), handles information. It applies to the iPhone and iPad app and to this website.

Your documents stay on your device

Every PDF you import, create or edit is stored in the app's own storage on your iPhone or iPad. This includes your files and folders, saved signatures, notes, stamps, drafts and settings. We do not run a server for Pagefill, so none of this is uploaded to us.

If your device backs up to iCloud or your computer, those backups may include the app's data. That is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and we cannot read it.

On-device processing

Text recognition (OCR), automatic detection for redaction (for example phone numbers, ID numbers and faces), conversions and the AI summary all run on your device using Apple's built-in frameworks. The document content is not sent anywhere to do this.

What we don't collect

  • No account, name, email address or password
  • No analytics, usage statistics or advertising identifiers
  • No ads and no third-party tracking or advertising SDKs
  • No location data. The metadata tools only read the files you choose, on your device

Sharing a document

When you share, email, print or save a document to another app or service, you choose where it goes. From then on, that app or service handles the file under its own privacy policy.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple. We never see your name, payment card or Apple ID. Apple tells the app whether you have an active subscription so it can turn on Pro features. Apple also notifies us when a subscription renews, expires or is refunded, without telling us who you are.

Refund requests. When someone asks Apple for a refund, Apple may ask us about the purchase. By subscribing, you agree that we may then tell Apple that the purchase was delivered, that a free version or trial was available beforehand, and that we prefer a refund of the unused part. We share nothing else, and only for purchases made after this consent appeared on the subscription screen.

Contacting support

If you email us or use Send Feedback in the app, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to send. When you report a problem you can attach a diagnostic log. You can read it before sending, and it contains app events, not your documents. We use this only to answer you and fix problems, and we delete it when it is no longer needed. Nothing is sent until you tap Send.

Children

Pagefill is a general productivity app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

You can delete any document, folder or saved signature in the app at any time. If you have emailed us, write to essentialdevteam@gmail.com and we will delete that correspondence. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about this page go to Essential Dev Team at essentialdevteam@gmail.com. We answer every message.
